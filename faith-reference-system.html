FAITH SYSTEM

Forge of Legends — Developer Reference

Overview

Each player picks one of 7 Deities at character creation. Their Faith Level (a single integer starting at 0) increases by +1 per sacrifice, consuming 1 of each of that deity's 3 worship materials. Faith Level drives two parallel progressions: Mantles (major tiers, 7 total) and Choirs (3 sub-tiers within each Mantle). Reaching a new Choir or Mantle increases the player's blessing multiplier. The base blessing effect for all deities is 3%, scaled by the current effective multiplier = Mantle base × Choir bonus.

Sacrifices Required — Mantle to Mantle

Each sacrifice = +1 Faith. Numbers below are total sacrifices needed to enter each new Mantle from the previous one's entry point (C1), and from C3 of the prior Mantle.

Mantle entry thresholds & gaps

0→ 30 Faithless C1 → Initiate C1 (30 sacrifices)
30→ 80 Initiate C1 → Devotee C1 (50 sacrifices)
80→ 180 Devotee C1 → Acolyte C1 (100 sacrifices)
180→ 380 Acolyte C1 → Zealot C1 (200 sacrifices)
380→ 780 Zealot C1 → Chosen C1 (400 sacrifices)
780→ 1580 Chosen C1 → High Priest C1 (800 sacrifices)
Pattern: Each Mantle's C1 threshold is exactly previous Mantle's C3 × 2 (15×2=30, 40×2=80, 90×2=180, 190×2=380, 390×2=780, 790×2=1580). So each gap roughly doubles, making early faith cheap and late faith an enormous grind.

Sacrifices Required — Choir to Choir

Within every Mantle, Choir thresholds are spaced exactly 5 Faith apart. That means every Choir advance costs exactly 5 sacrifices regardless of Mantle.

Within-Mantle choir gaps (same for all mantles)

C1→ C2 (+5 Faith = 5 sacrifices)
C2→ C3 (+5 Faith = 5 sacrifices)
So entering a Mantle at C1 and reaching C3 costs just 10 more sacrifices. The real cost is always the gap between mantles.
Total sacrifices to max (High Priest C3 = Faith 1590): 1,590 sacrifices. Since each sacrifice consumes 1 of each of 3 materials (3 unique items per deity), that's 4,770 worship material items consumed to fully max faith.

Blessing Multiplier Breakdown

Base blessing for all deities is 3%. Effective blessing = 3% × effectiveMult. Effective multiplier = Mantle base mult + (choir × 0.1).

Mantle No Choir Choir 1 (+0.1) Choir 2 (+0.2) Choir 3 (+0.3) Max Bless %
Faithless ×1.0 → 3% ×1.1 → 3% ×1.2 → 4% ×1.3 → 4% ~3.9%
Initiate ×1.5 → 5% ×1.6 → 5% ×1.7 → 5% ×1.8 → 5% ~5.4%
Devotee ×2.0 → 6% ×2.1 → 6% ×2.2 → 7% ×2.3 → 7% ~6.9%
Acolyte ×3.0 → 9% ×3.1 → 9% ×3.2 → 10% ×3.3 → 10% ~9.9%
Zealot ×4.0 → 12% ×4.1 → 12% ×4.2 → 13% ×4.3 → 13% ~12.9%
Chosen ×5.0 → 15% ×5.1 → 15% ×5.2 → 16% ×5.3 → 16% ~15.9%
High Priest ×6.0 → 18% ×6.1 → 18% ×6.2 → 19% ×6.3 → 19% ~18.9%
Rounding: _getBlessingDesc uses Math.round(3 × mult), so e.g. ×1.1 → 3.3 → rounds to 3%, ×2.2 → 6.6 → rounds to 7%. The raw value used in calculations (e.g. Veil's EXP bonus, profession luck) is _getFaithBlessingPct which returns the unrounded decimal: (3 × mult) / 100.

Miscellaneous Rules

Shrine locations: Must be physically present at your deity's shrine to sacrifice. Shrine of Secrets → Veil | Aurora Basin → Mah'run | Forgotten Estuary → Alistor | Purgatory of Light → Sah'run | Temple of Verdict → Arion | Heart Garden → Freyja | Valley of Overflowing → Elionidas.
Divine Shift Potion (15,000 coins): Lets a player change deity, but resets Faith Level to 0. All Mantle and Choir progress is lost.
Advancement ingredients: Separate from worship materials. These are bestowed via events/achievements and scale ×2 per rank (1x → 2x → 4x → …). They gate advancement, not Faith Level itself.
Veil's blessing in code: Used directly in the gathering gather loop as _veilBonus = 1 + _getFaithBlessingPct(charData), applied to both item XP gain and profession XP. It's one of the few blessings that has a hard mechanical effect in current code.
